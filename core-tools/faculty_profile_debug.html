Azarnoosh, Jimmy

Dr. Jimmy Azarnoosh is a faculty member in the Biocomputational Engineering (BCE) program. His expertise lies in machine learning, image processing, and computational modeling and simulation in bioengineering. Before joining UMD, he worked as a Research Engineer at the Northeast Georgia Health System. He completed his postdoctoral training in Biomedical Engineering Department at the Medical College of Wisconsin and Marquette University. Dr. Azarnoosh earned his Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Texas at Dallas (UTD).

Machine Learning/AI, Computational Methods (CFD, FEA, and FSI), Image Processing
